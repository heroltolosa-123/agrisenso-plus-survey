Recording a non-answer

These mean four different things in the analysis. Collapsing them into one is the most damaging habit in the field.

Does not know

Choose "Don't know" or "Unable to estimate" if offered, otherwise leave blank

Declines to answer

Choose "Prefer not to answer" if offered, otherwise leave blank

The answer is nothing

Enter 0, or tick "None". Never leave it blank

Does not apply

The app greys it out for you. Do not look for a way to answer it

Never type "N/A" into a text box. If it truly does not apply, the app has already skipped it.
