What the app does not do

It does not check the respondent against the LANDBANK list

Borrower type, segment, name and sampling-frame ID are whatever you enter. Verify them on paper before you start.

It does not catch a wrong but plausible number

Typing 5 hectares instead of 0.5 is accepted silently.

It does not hold two interviews at once

One in-progress response per instrument, per device. Finish and submit before starting the next.

It does not upload until you have signal

Submitted is not the same as synced. Press Sync Pending Now.

Know these before the field, not during
