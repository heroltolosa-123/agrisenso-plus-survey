LEARNED THE HARD WAY

Two ways data has been lost

Answering "Cancel" on the resume prompt

Opening an instrument with a saved response asks whether to resume it. Cancel starts a blank form and the saved answers are gone.

Read that prompt every time. If unsure, resume — you can always leave and come back.

Starting a second interview too early

There is one draft per instrument per device. Beginning a new Instrument A interview while another is in progress overwrites it.

Finish and submit first. Never hand the device to another enumerator mid-interview.

Never clear browsing or site data — that erases queued responses
