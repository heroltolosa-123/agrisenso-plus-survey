The supervisor's job

The quality-control fields belong to the supervisor and the data manager. An enumerator marking their own interview verified defeats the control.

EVERY DAY

Every enumerator has synced — nothing queued on a device overnight

Response count in the Sheet matches interviews reported

Every stopped or refused case reported, replacements drawn

Spot-check three records against the sampling frame

Watch for zero-heavy records — usually fillers, not real values

EVERY WEEK

Set Back-check Status on the selected sample

Review interview durations — a 35-minute Instrument A interview did not ask every question properly

Confirm enumerator name spellings are still consistent

Corrections are made in the Sheet, never by submitting a second record — that creates a duplicate

Supervisor Verification and Data Verification open at "Pending"
