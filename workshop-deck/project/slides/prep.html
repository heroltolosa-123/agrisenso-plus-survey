Before you leave for the barangay

ON THE DEVICE, WITH SIGNAL

Open the survey once so the questionnaire downloads — it will not open cold with no signal the first time.

Check both instruments appear on the chooser screen.

Check there is no leftover draft. If it asks to resume, settle that with your supervisor first.

Charge the device fully. Ninety minutes with the screen on is a real load.

ON PAPER, IN HAND

The approved sampling frame extract: frame ID, name, borrower type, segment, barangay.

A printed consent script, in case the device fails mid-interview.

Your supervisor's number, for the cases the app tells you to refer.
