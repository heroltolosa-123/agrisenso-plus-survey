THE INTERVIEW STOPS HERE

When the app stops you

A red panel appears and Next is disabled. Press End interview and submit this record — submitting is how the case is counted in the sample disposition. Never abandon it.

TRIGGER WHAT TO DO
Consent refusedThank the respondent and close
A — no loan agreement on recordRefer to supervisor
A — agreement cannot be verifiedStop before any substantive question; refer for validation
B — has, or has had, an AGRISENSO loanBelongs in the borrower sample; arrange a replacement
Respondent under 18Cannot be interviewed; arrange a replacement
Representative not authorisedFind an authorised person, or book a callback

Call your supervisor the same day for any stop other than a refusal
