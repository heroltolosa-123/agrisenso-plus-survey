Introduction and Informed Consent

Consent to Participate required

Do you voluntarily agree to participate?

Yes – Proceed  ·  No – End interview and thank respondent

What it measures. The legal basis for the interview. Everything that follows depends on it.

How to ask it. Read the consent script in full, word for word, inserting your own name where indicated. Then ask the question.

Watch for. No ends the interview immediately. Thank the respondent, submit the record so the refusal is counted in the sample disposition, and do not attempt to persuade them.

Consent for Possible Validation / Back-check required

A “No” response does not affect participation in this interview.

May the research team contact you again, if necessary, for a brief validation or back-check?

Yes  ·  No

What it measures. Permission for a later validation call or back-check.

How to ask it. Ask plainly, after consent to participate.

Watch for. Say clearly that No here does not affect the present interview. Respondents often assume refusing will end the interview.

Consent Confirmation required

Enumerator Attestation

I certify that I read/explained the informed consent and that the respondent voluntarily agreed to participate.

Respondent Signature (optional)

What it measures. Your certification that consent was properly obtained.

How to ask it. You tick the attestation, not the respondent. Offer the signature line; never insist.

Watch for. Only tick it if you genuinely read or explained the consent. A respondent who agreed verbally but will not sign is still a valid interview.
