Instrument B — Non-Borrower Comparison Group

AGRISENSO Plus Baseline Study — paper questionnaire, version 1.0 (2026-08-27)

This is the paper form. Fieldwork is done in the webapp; print this for review, for briefings, and as the fallback when a device fails. Every item, option and rule here is generated from the live questionnaire, so the two cannot drift.

Paper has no routing. The app skips what does not apply. On paper the shaded line above an item tells you when to ask it — Ask only if A5 = Yes — and a red box tells you when to stop. Follow them, or the form collects answers the dataset will reject.

Blank is a valid answer for everything except the eligibility and consent items. Never write a filler to fill a space.

☐ one answer  ·  Tick all that apply above a list means more than one may be ticked  ·  items marked for office use are completed by the app, not by hand.
