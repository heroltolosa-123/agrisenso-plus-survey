Introduction and Informed Consent

Consent to Participate required

Do you voluntarily agree to participate in this survey?

Yes – Proceed  ·  No – End interview and thank respondent

What it measures. The legal basis for the interview. Everything that follows depends on it.

How to ask it. Read the consent script in full, word for word, inserting your own name where indicated. Then ask the question.

Watch for. No ends the interview immediately. Thank the respondent, submit the record so the refusal is counted in the sample disposition, and do not attempt to persuade them.

Consent for Possible Validation / Back-check required

A “No” response does not affect participation in the present interview.

May the research team contact you again, if necessary, for a brief validation or back-check related to this survey?

Yes  ·  No

What it measures. Permission for a later validation call or back-check.

How to ask it. Ask plainly, after consent to participate.

Watch for. Say clearly that No here does not affect the present interview. Respondents often assume refusing will end the interview.

Consent Confirmation required

Enumerator Attestation

I certify that I read/explained the informed consent and that the respondent voluntarily agreed to participate.

Respondent Signature (optional)

What it measures. Your certification that consent was properly obtained.

How to ask it. You tick the attestation, not the respondent. Offer the signature line; never insist.

Watch for. Only tick it if you genuinely read or explained the consent. A respondent who agreed verbally but will not sign is still a valid interview.

A5AGRISENSO Plus Loan Agreement Verification required

What it measures. Whether an AGRISENSO Plus loan agreement exists on the Program record. A hard gate.

A6Status of Loan Release required

Enumerator Note: This item is important for routing. Questions concerning actual loan utilization and experience after receiving financing apply only where loan proceeds have already been released.

Under the existing AGRISENSO Plus loan agreement, has any portion of the approved loan already been released/disbursed?

Yes, fully released  ·  Yes, partially released  ·  No, not yet released  ·  Respondent does not know / requires verification

What it measures. Whether loan proceeds have been released. Decides whether the utilisation section is asked.

How to ask it. Ask how much of the approved loan has actually been released or disbursed.

Watch for. 'Not yet released' skips the whole loan-utilisation section, correctly, because there are no proceeds to discuss. Do not answer this on the respondent's behalf.

B5Household Size

Including yourself, how many persons usually live in your household? Total household members (persons)

Only when A2 Borrower Type = Individual borrower; between 1 and 50; whole number; in persons

What it measures. Household size, the denominator for the two questions that follow.

How to ask it. 'Including yourself, how many persons usually live in your household?'

Watch for. Minimum 1, because the respondent counts. 'Usually live' means regular members: include a student who comes home, exclude a long-term overseas worker unless the household treats them as resident.
